VISUAL IDENTITY · v3 · 2026

01 — Concept

Open is the only verb.

Oculis cannot edit, format or save. The one thing it does is open. So the identity is built on a single gesture, the moment of opening one's eyes: nothing is loaded, something arrives, you are reading. The same gesture is the mark, the motion and the product's empty state.

02 — Mark

An open eye, on a 3 : 4 : 5 triangle.

Each lid is an arc of the same circle. Placing the circles so that center, eye corner and midpoint form a 3 : 4 : 5 right triangle gives an almond exactly twice as wide as it is tall, with every key point on a whole number of the grid.

3 : 4 : 5

Center to corner, half width, radius. Scaled by 6: 18, 24, 30.

Lids

Two arcs of radius 30, centers at (32,14) and (32,50). Corners land on (8,32) and (56,32), sharp miter joins.

Almond

48 × 24 on the stroke centerline, exactly 2 : 1, centered on the grid at (32,32).

Pupil

A full circle of radius 2u, centered, looking straight at you. It is the only brand color in the mark.

Stroke

u = 4. The lids keep the 1 : 2 stroke-to-pupil ratio from v2.

03 — Ideas

Three ways to open.

Iterations within the same concept. A carries the system, B and C are kept as options.

04 — Motion

Only the upper lid moves.

The pupil is clipped by the almond, so a lowering lid covers it the way a real eyelid would. Closing lays the upper lid onto the lower one. No scaling, no fading, one curve in motion.

Blink · 4 s loop · 240 ms close

Hover the “Dragging” card to play the open and close.

05 — Lockup

As tall as the l.

The wordmark is drawn from circles and stems with the same stroke as the eye. In the lockup the eye stands on the baseline and rises to the ascender, the height of the l.

06 — App icon

Wide awake.

On the icon the almond is filled white and the pupil is cut out in Iris, so the tile looks back at you. A solid shape holds up at 16 px without a separate small drawing.

07 — Color

Iris, and everything else stays GitHub.

Documents render in GitHub's own neutrals, so the brand owns exactly one color. Iris is an electric ultramarine in the spirit of International Klein Blue, brightened for screens. It is the pupil, the icon field, and nowhere inside a document.
